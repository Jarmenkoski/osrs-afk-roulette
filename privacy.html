Privacy

AFK Roulette website, Discord bot and RuneLite plugin · updated 9 October 2026

Who runs this

AFK Roulette is a free, non-commercial fan project. Questions and data requests: open an issue on GitHub or ask in the group's Discord. The server (afk-api.rosu.fi) is hosted in Finland, in the EU.

What is stored, and why

  • Your RuneScape name and task history — tasks rolled, done and skipped, streaks, quests you marked done. Needed for tasks, streaks and highscores.
  • Your levels, looked up from the official OSRS Hiscores (or Wise Old Man) by your name, cached for 30 minutes.
  • Task suggestions and votes you make on the website, with your name.
  • Discord: if you link a name with the bot, your Discord user id and that name.
  • RuneLite plugin, only after you turn on Connect to AFK Roulette server: your IP address (as with any web request), your RuneScape name and your task results. Only if you also turn on Share my data with the group: your levels, xp, quest states, inventory, equipment, bank, seed vault, current world and an online heartbeat, visible to people who have your group's token.
  • Server logs keep IP addresses and requested addresses (which can include names) for 14 days, for security and troubleshooting.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else. The website keeps your name, group token and history in your own browser (localStorage).

How long, and how to remove it

  • Turning off Share my data with the group, or pressing Leave group in the plugin, removes your shared data from that group right away. For groups made in the plugin, leaving also removes your task history in that group.
  • Everything else is kept while the service runs. Ask (see above) and we remove your name's data.

Under the EU GDPR you can ask to see, correct or remove your data, and complain to your data protection authority (in Finland, the Data Protection Ombudsman).

Services we talk to

OSRS Hiscores (Jagex) and Wise Old Man for levels, Discord for the bot and channel posts, and the OSRS Wiki for links and images. Their own privacy policies apply to them.
